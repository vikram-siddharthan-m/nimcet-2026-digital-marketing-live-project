# Campaign Architecture

This document summarises the updated campaign structure and working assumptions recorded in the project materials.

## Confidentiality note

Only permitted campaign-level details are included in this public repository. Intricate account details such as account identifiers, audience lists, exact keyword sets, targeting configurations, bid controls, internal optimisation logs, and applicant-level data are confidential.

## Core campaign logic

The live project notes structure the marketing effort into five phases:

|Phase|Objective|Google Ads direction|Organic content direction|
|-|-|-|-|
|Awareness Campaign|Inform students that registration had started|Broad MCA and NIMCET awareness-led discovery|Posters on the exam, participating institutes, exam date; authority-led explainer content|
|Opportunity Campaign|Show the career value of MCA through NIMCET|Reach MCA aspirants and career-motivated prospects|Infographics on IT careers, salary potential, alumni success, and programme value|
|Consideration Campaign|Convince students to register|Capture higher-intent applicants considering the exam|Testimonials, preparation tips, exam pattern, application support|
|Urgency Campaign|Remind students that the deadline was approaching|Deadline-sensitive messaging and higher-intent traffic capture|Countdown posters, FAQ refreshers, application reminders|
|Final Push Campaign|Capture last-minute applicants|Final-window action-oriented messaging|Daily reminders, quick application guide, final call-to-apply creatives|

## Campaign architecture interpretation

The updated dashboard evidence supports a stronger campaign-architecture narrative:

1. **The campaign was staged rather than random.**  
Each phase had a distinct communication role.
2. **The later windows were performance-heavy.**  
Campaigns 3, 4, and 5 show high click and impression volumes around the deadline and final-push period.
3. **The final window was highly efficient.**  
Campaign 5 shows **39.21K clicks**, **277.52K impressions**, **14.13% CTR**, and **₹0.24 Avg. CPC** in the permitted dashboard evidence.
4. **The campaign crossed the outcome target.**  
The overall registration objective was **18,000 registrations**, and the cycle crossed **19,000+ registrations**.

## Content themes noted in the working files

* student, faculty, and alumni stories
* infographics
* programme-value content
* job and career opportunity content
* application-flow explainers
* FAQs
* deadline reminders
* official, professional communication rather than trend-driven posts

## Working assumptions and explicit decisions

The project material records a few operational decisions:

* the live project should remain a digital marketing project rather than an applicant-follow-up or counselling-tracking exercise
* the campaign should follow application psychology flow
* existing institutional channels were more suitable than creating a temporary new handle for a single-year cycle
* the tone should remain professional, not casual or trend-led
* public documentation should show only permitted details
