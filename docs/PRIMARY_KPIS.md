# Primary KPIs

This document isolates the primary performance KPIs that were visible and defensible in the updated Google Ads dashboard evidence.

## Why the KPI layer is intentionally narrow

This public case study is built from redacted screenshots and supporting project notes rather than a full ad-account export. Because of that, the KPI layer is limited to metrics that are clearly visible, reproducible, and safe to interpret in public.

## Confidentiality boundary

Only permitted campaign details are shown. Other intricate data including account identifiers, keyword-level details, audience lists, detailed targeting settings, budget-control logic, private conversion paths, and applicant-level records are confidential.

## Primary KPIs tracked

|KPI|Why it mattered|
|-|-|
|Impressions|To measure campaign visibility and top-of-funnel reach|
|Clicks|To measure traffic generated from paid media|
|CTR|To understand how effectively impressions converted into engagement|
|Avg. CPC|To understand visible traffic-acquisition efficiency|
|Campaign-wise clicks|To compare campaign-stage response volume|
|Campaign-wise impressions|To compare campaign-stage reach|
|Campaign-wise CTR|To compare engagement efficiency by campaign|
|Campaign-wise Avg. CPC|To compare traffic efficiency by campaign|
|Registration target achievement|To document whether the overall cycle objective was crossed|
