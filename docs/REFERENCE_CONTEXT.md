## Eligibility summary from the official brochure

Eligible candidates included Indian nationals with:

* at least 60% aggregate marks or 6.5 CGPA on a 10-point scale
* 55% or 6.0 CGPA on a 10-point scale in the case of SC/ST/PwD candidates
* either a minimum three-year UG programme with Mathematics or Statistics as one of the subjects
* or a B.E. / B.Tech. degree from a recognised university or institution
