## Interpretation

### 1\. Engagement quality was strong

The visible CTRs are consistently strong across the updated campaign evidence. Campaign 2 and Campaign 5 crossed **14% CTR**, indicating strong response relative to impressions.

### 2\. Traffic acquisition became highly cost-efficient

Campaign 5 delivered the lowest visible Avg. CPC at **₹0.24**, followed by Campaign 2 at **₹0.27**. This supports a strong efficiency story in the permitted dashboard evidence.

### 3\. Late-stage urgency appears important

Campaigns 3, 4, and 5 were concentrated around the final application and immediate late-stage period. Their high click and impression levels are consistent with deadline-driven applicant behaviour.

### 4\. The public evidence should be framed carefully

The screenshots support claims about:

* visible impressions
* visible clicks
* visible CTR
* visible Avg. CPC
* campaign-stage performance
* overall registration target achievement

The screenshots do not support public disclosure of:

* keyword-level performance
* audience-level performance
* applicant-level attribution
* cost per completed application
* private conversion-path data
* internal optimisation logs
