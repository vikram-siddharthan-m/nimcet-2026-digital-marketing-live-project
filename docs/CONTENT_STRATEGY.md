# Content Strategy

This document packages the organic content direction visible in the project notes and aligns it with the updated campaign evidence.

## Strategic role of organic content

In this project, organic content had to do more than generate awareness. It also needed to:

* explain the exam clearly
* reduce information friction
* build authority and trust
* make the application process feel manageable
* reinforce urgency as the deadline approached
* support final-window action

## Confidentiality note

Only permitted public-facing content strategy details are included. Intricate campaign details, internal creative decisions, private performance exports, audience-level details, and applicant-level data are confidential.

## Core informational content buckets

### 1\. What NIMCET is

Content in this bucket should answer:

* what the exam is
* who coordinates it
* which institutes participate
* why it matters

### 2\. Eligibility and fit

Content in this bucket should help students quickly assess:

* whether their academic background qualifies
* whether they should seriously consider the MCA route
* what documentation or preparation they need

### 3\. Dates and deadlines

This is deadline-protection content:

* registration open
* application deadline
* admit card window
* exam date
* counselling milestones

### 4\. Programme value

This is aspiration and future-outcome content:

* career pathways after MCA
* industry relevance
* institute reputation
* student or alumni outcomes where publishable

### 5\. Application support

This is friction-reduction content:

* registration-flow explainers
* quick FAQs
* reminders
* what to keep ready before applying

### 6\. Urgency and final push

This is deadline-action content:

* countdown creatives
* final call-to-apply posts
* last-date reminders
* quick application guidance
* reassurance-led FAQs

## Format ideas explicitly noted in the files

* posters

* success stories
* countdown creatives
* FAQ posts

## Tone and quality rules implied by the notes

The working material suggests several useful content guardrails:

* keep the tone professional
* avoid unserious or trend-driven content
* use motivating but relatable proof points
* avoid making average aspirants feel excluded
* prioritise clarity over hype
* lean on institutional authority where possible
* show only permitted details publicly

## Suggested content sequencing

### Early stage

* exam introduction
* eligibility
* participating institutes
* important dates

### Mid stage

* MCA career value
* institute and programme credibility
* testimonials or authority-led explainers
* application support content

### Late stage

* countdown reminders
* FAQ refreshers
* final call-to-apply creatives
* simple, low-friction guidance posts

## Why this matters

For an exam-led campaign, users often move from:

**awareness → self-check → reassurance → urgency → application**

The content plan in the project materials is strongest when it supports that exact flow.
